# EDUBRIDGE

---

## What EduBridge actually is:

Imagine a student named Rahul.

- Rahul belongs to a financially struggling family.
- He wants to continue his engineering education.
- His family cannot afford his college fees.
- A person named Priya wants to sponsor deserving students.
- However, Priya doesn't know which students genuinely need financial support.

There is a problem:

How can Rahul prove his financial situation, and how can Priya confidently find and support him?

That's exactly the problem EduBridge tries to solve.

---

## What does EduBridge do?

EduBridge connects four different types of users:

1. **Student:** Requests financial support for education.
2. **Volunteer:** Performs preliminary verification of the student's documents and information.
3. **Admin:** Reviews applications, considers verification and AI assessment results, and makes the final eligibility decision.
4. **Sponsor:** Discovers eligible students and provides financial support.

The platform manages the entire process, from registration to sponsorship tracking.

---

## What makes EduBridge different?

| Feature | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Application management | Students apply for educational support through a structured process. |
| Document verification | Checks the authenticity and validity of submitted documents using appropriate verification methods. |
| Volunteer verification | Humans review student information and identify inconsistencies. |
| AI need assessment | Estimates the student's financial need and generates a recommendation. |
| Sponsor matching | Recommends suitable eligible students based on sponsor preferences. |
| Sponsorship management | Manages sponsorship commitments, allocations and records. |
| Audit and analytics | Tracks important activities and provides platform statistics. |

One important principle:

**AI assists decisions; it does not replace human administrative authority.**

This means AI can analyse information and recommend decisions, but the final eligibility approval belongs to an administrator.

## Problem Statement:

### Problems faced by students:

Many students cannot continue their education because of financial difficulties.

For example:

- College fees are too expensive.
- Parents have low incomes.
- There are multiple children studying in the same family.
- Students cannot afford books, accommodation or other educational expenses.

Such students need financial assistance.

### Problems faced by sponsors:

Suppose a sponsor has ₹1,00,000 available for supporting students.

The sponsor faces several questions:

- How do I find students who genuinely need help?
- How do I know whether their documents are genuine?
- How much financial support does each student require?
- Which students should I prioritize?
- How do I track where my money is allocated?

Without a proper platform, this becomes difficult.

### How EduBridge solves these problems:

EduBridge provides a common platform where:

- Students submit structured applications.
- Volunteers perform preliminary verification.
- Administrators review applications and make final eligibility decisions.
- AI helps assess financial needs.
- Sponsors discover eligible students through recommendations.
- Sponsorship commitments and allocations are recorded and tracked.
- Important activities are maintained through audit logs.

EduBridge solves the trust and coordination problem between students and sponsors.

## Goals:

Your project has eight main goals.

1. **Structure educational support applications:** Provide a standard application process instead of collecting information randomly.
2. **Improve trust through verification:** Check submitted documents and application information using official, institutional or manual verification methods.
3. **Reduce manual effort using AI:** Automate document processing where appropriate and assist need assessment.
4. **Help sponsors discover students:** Recommend eligible students according to sponsor preferences.
5. **Make sponsorship traceable:** Record sponsorship commitments, allocations and remaining requirements.
6. **Maintain audit trails:** Keep records of important activities performed by students, volunteers, administrators and sponsors.
7. **Protect student information:** Prevent unauthorized access to private information through secure storage, consent and role-based access control.
8. **Provide modular architecture:** Divide the project into independent, manageable modules.

---

## Non-Goals:

| Non-goal | Simple explanation |
|---|---|
| AI automatically deciding funding | Humans must make final administrative decisions. |
| OCR proving authenticity | OCR only extracts text; it cannot prove that a document is genuine. |
| Replacing human verification | Volunteers and administrators remain important. |
| Depending entirely on DigiLocker | The system must work even without production DigiLocker API access. |
| Building unnecessary DL models | Use simpler solutions when sufficient. |
| Exposing private information | Student privacy must be protected. |
| Starting with microservices | Begin with a simpler modular architecture. |
| Building payment infrastructure early | First stabilize sponsorship business logic and transaction records. |
| Making Aadhaar mandatory | Students should have alternative identity-verification methods. |

## ML Algorithms:

ML algorithms will mainly be used for student need assessment.

| Algorithm | Use in EduBridge | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Logistic Regression | Need classification | Baseline model |
| Decision Tree | Need classification | Explainable model |
| Random Forest | Need classification | Strong candidate |
| XGBoost | Need classification | Advanced comparison |
| SVM | Need classification | Optional comparison |
| Linear Regression | Support-gap prediction | Only if reliable numerical training labels exist |

### How ML will be used:

The system considers relevant information such as:

- Annual family income.
- Number of family members.
- Number of earning members.
- Annual educational expenses.
- Existing financial assistance.
- Requested financial support.
- Other relevant financial circumstances.

The model generates a financial-need score or priority recommendation.

For example:

- High financial need.
- Medium financial need.
- Low financial need.

### Recommended ML implementation:

1. Start with a rule-based need assessment.
2. Collect reliable and appropriately consented historical application data.
3. Establish clear target labels through reviewed administrative decisions.
4. Train and compare Logistic Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest.
5. Evaluate the models using Precision, Recall, F1-score and Confusion Matrix.
6. Introduce XGBoost only if it provides meaningful improvement.
7. Display the assessment result and relevant factors to the administrator.

**Important:** Do not train a model using randomly generated data and present its predictions as genuine financial-need assessments. ML recommendations must be evaluated for accuracy, fairness and bias.

## Documents Verification:

The main documents required from students are:

1. Income Certificate.
2. College ID Card.
3. Latest Academic Marksheet.


### Application 1:

**EduBridge**

The main platform where students apply for educational sponsorship.

### Application 2:

**EduVault — Your simulated DigiLocker**

A separate application that stores sample academic documents and allows students to access and share them with EduBridge after providing consent.

EduVault is a DigiLocker-inspired testing environment developed specifically for the EduBridge project. It is not an official DigiLocker service.

### EduVault Roles:

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Student | Accesses and shares academic documents. |
| College / Issuer | Uploads and issues academic marksheets. |
| EduBridge | Requests and verifies academic documents. |
| EduVault Admin | Manages institutions, students and test records. |

### EduVault Workflow:

**1. College creates an account**

The college registers as an issuer and gets an institution account.

**2. College uploads academic records**

It uploads sample marksheets with:

- Student name.
- Roll number.
- University name.
- Semester.
- Academic year.
- Marks.
- Result.

**3. College issues the marksheet**

EduVault generates a document hash using SHA-256 and creates a digital signature using the college's registered private key.

The document, signature and issuer information are stored securely.

**4. Student accesses EduVault**

The student logs in and sees the academic marksheets issued by their college.

**5. Student applies for sponsorship on EduBridge**

They select "Verify using EduVault".

**6. EduBridge requests document access**

EduVault displays a consent screen showing which marksheet EduBridge wants to access and why it needs the document.

**7. Student grants permission**

EduVault generates a temporary, one-time document-sharing reference.

**8. EduBridge retrieves the marksheet**

Its backend requests the document from EduVault and validates the digital signature using the registered college's public key.

**9. Verification result is saved**

EduBridge records the issuer, document ID, signature verification result, consent reference and timestamp.

### Important points:

- A valid digital signature confirms document integrity and that it was signed using the registered issuer's private key.
- It does not independently prove that the original marks entered by an issuer are truthful.
- Only registered and trusted institutions should be allowed to issue documents.
- Test marksheets and student records must be fictional and clearly labelled as test data.
- EduVault is intended for academic marksheet verification. Other student documents require separate verification methods.

## Other Document Verification Methods:

### 1. Income Certificate Verification

The student uploads the income certificate and provides the certificate/application number.

Verification process:

1. Student uploads the income certificate.
2. Student enters the certificate or application number.
3. Volunteer or administrator opens the official Andhra Pradesh MeeSeva verification facility.
4. The certificate details are checked against the official portal.
5. The administrator compares the details with the student's application.
6. The verification result is recorded.

Official portal:

https://onlineap.meeseva.gov.in/CitizenPortal/UserInterface/Citizen/Home.aspx

If an authorised government verification API becomes available, it can be integrated later.

### 2. College ID Verification

Verification process:

1. Student uploads the college ID card.
2. Student enters the college name, university name and roll number.
3. EduBridge checks whether the institution is registered.
4. The volunteer or administrator contacts the college or uses its official verification facility.
5. The college confirms the student's current enrolment.
6. The verification result is recorded.

**Future enhancement:** Colleges can register directly on EduBridge and maintain their student enrolment records, making verification faster.

## Sponsor Matching:

After an application receives final administrative eligibility approval, the student becomes available for sponsor matching.

The system considers:

- Sponsor's available budget.
- Preferred education level.
- Preferred support category.
- Student's financial-need priority.
- Requested financial assistance.
- Remaining financial requirement.

### Recommended approach:

Start with rule-based filtering and weighted scoring.

Example:

| Matching factor | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Education level match | 30% |
| Support category match | 30% |
| Budget compatibility | 25% |
| Sponsor preferences | 15% |

Match Score = (0.30 × Education Match) + (0.30 × Category Match) + (0.25 × Budget Match) + (0.15 × Preference Match)

The system recommends suitable students to sponsors.

The sponsor can then review the permitted student profile and choose whom to support.

Private student information must never be exposed merely because the matching algorithm uses it internally.

## Sponsorship Management:

Once a sponsor selects a student:

1. Sponsor selects the student and support amount.
2. System checks the remaining financial requirement.
3. Sponsorship record is created.
4. Student and sponsor can view the sponsorship status.
5. Admin can monitor the sponsorship.
6. Every sponsorship activity is recorded in the audit logs.

The system must prevent duplicate or excessive sponsorship allocations.

**Important business rule:**

Total confirmed sponsorship amount must not exceed the student's approved financial requirement.

Actual payment gateway integration and fund disbursement infrastructure can be introduced in a later phase.

- Students eligible for sponsorship.

## Security and Privacy:

EduBridge handles sensitive student and financial information. Therefore, security is an important requirement.

The platform should implement:

1. Secure password hashing.
2. JWT-based authentication.
3. Role-Based Access Control.
4. Secure document storage.
5. Student consent before sharing documents.
6. Restricted access to private student information.
7. Audit logging.
8. Secure API communication using HTTPS.
9. Document access controls and temporary sharing references.
10. Appropriate document retention and deletion policies.

Sensitive information such as Aadhaar numbers, bank details and private financial records must not be unnecessarily exposed or stored.

## Final Definition of EduBridge:

EduBridge is an AI-assisted educational sponsorship platform that connects financially struggling students with sponsors through structured applications, document verification, human review, intelligent need assessment and transparent sponsorship management.

Trust through verification, fairness through assessment, transparency through tracking and human authority through final administrative decisions.
